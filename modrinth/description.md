# Pickup Cat

A cat hangs from the top of your screen and becomes your **item pickup log**.

- Pick up an item -> the cat **eats** it
- Drop an item -> the cat **throws it up**
- The cat **talks** in speech bubbles: it gets hungry when you stop picking things up, reacts to what it eats, and complains when you make it throw up
- Drop a **dye** and the cat falls down and throws a **party** with it
- A small log under the cat shows what you gained and lost

Made with **Hypixel SkyBlock** in mind: items are matched by their SkyBlock ID, so stat and lore changes never count as pickups. Works everywhere else too.

**Resize the cat in-game** with `]` / `[` (rebindable).

**Client-side only.** Needs Fabric API. Config: `config/pickupcat.json` (size, position, log, speed, Hypixel-only mode).
